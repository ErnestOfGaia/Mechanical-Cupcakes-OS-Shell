When you're not making food

Sanitize, organize, stock up, support, sweep.

Nothing else to do? Help somebody with a cleaning project. First three are for you and your station. Support and sweep are what you do for everybody else.

What this is not

It is not a test and it is not a record. Nobody is told what you looked at or what you got wrong. Nothing on the phone prints either — the cards are posted at the station, and the binder is the thing built for paper.

The printed binder — the cards for the wall, the teacher's aids and the manager's copies.
