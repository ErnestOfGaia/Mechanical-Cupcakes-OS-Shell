Point & sayOil to upper cold scribe mark. Hoods & makeup-air active. Wells 1-3 to 350°F; probe in ice-water (32°F ±1°F). Egg wash ice bath (≤ 40°F). Heat lamp bulbs (≥ 165°F).
CHEF →why it's done this way Q02.3

02. MID-SHIFT RESETI doWe doYou do
Point & saySkim every 30m in the rush. Filter Wells 1 & 2 mid-day. Top off max 1 qt per well. No aerosol sprays near vats. Freezer < 0°F; FIFO. Discard clumped dredge flour.
CHEF →why it's done this way Q02.4

03. CLOSE BREAKDOWNI doWe doYou do
Point & sayPower down burners & pilot valves; cool below 150°F. Lockout/tagout pilot toggle; do not drain boiling oil. Complete filtration; replace filter pad envelope.
WhyInstall clean stainless night covers over all 4 wells. Secures vats from airborne particulates overnight

ALSO TODAY · NOT ON THE CARDI doWe doYou do
Point & sayMid-shift and close both start the same way: change sanitizer buckets, add towels if needed, sweep if the floor is a hazard. Then: do I need to cover anyone so they can take a break? Any line items to stock up, and are the togo items stocked? If someone calls in sick tomorrow will we still be stocked up — tell the manager if we're low. Mid-shift only: are there opening duties left, and should I make food so the opener can finish theirs? Closing only: start with the areas that stay clean the longest, and prioritise getting dishes back to the dishwashers.
