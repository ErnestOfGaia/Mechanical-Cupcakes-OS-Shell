When you're not making food: ask, don't tellEvery answer is on the card
Where does the oil level sit at open? Upper cold scribe mark. Cold marker line; do not overfill past cold notch
What do you wipe down with, and what's banned near the vats? Use degreasing sanitizer wipe; no aerosol sprays near vats
What should the freezer read when you restock, and how do you rotate? Verify freezer readout < 0°F; FIFO inventory rotation
What temp do you cool below at close, and what do you never drain? Cool below 150°F. Lockout/tagout pilot toggle; do not drain boiling oil
At close, how do the sanitized baskets hang? Wire baskets inverted on wall bracket
