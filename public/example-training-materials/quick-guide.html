01. Storage zones & critical temps Card 01 · Section 01
01Zone A // Freezer
Frozen food storage
Target < 31°F (<1°C)
Fries · Ice cream · Onion rings · Bean patties · Shrimp · Cheese curds · GF pizza crust · GF buns · Chicken strips
If freezer is beeping: temp is low, notify chef, keep closed.
02Zone B // Line fridge
Cold food storage
Target < 41°F (<5°C)
Fish · Cocktail sauce · Tartar sauce · Coleslaw · Coleslaw dressing · Backup milk · Seasonal items
Allergen: Use gloves and appropriate containers to stay safe.
03Zone C // Fryer
Well submersion & timing
Wells 1–3: 350°F (177°C)
Drop away from torso. Agitate basket at 30s elapsed. Max 50% basket load.
Ready or not: If Fryer is Not Ready do not drop batch. Temp drop >25°F = basket overload. Refire batch.
04Zone D // Calamari fridge
Calamari fridge storage
Discard timer: 7 min max hold
Calamari · Shrimp · Peppers · Milk · Seafood for another station · Seasonal: tomato, lettuce
Heat lamp min 165°F (74°C). Reset KDS timer on dump. Pitch batch at the 7:00 mark.
05Zone E // Warmer cabinet & fry holder
Min hold temp 145°F (63°C)
Fry holder: do not hold fries longer than 20 min. · Warmer cabinet: chicken and tortillas hold no longer than 4 hours.

02. Shift checklists & routines Card 02 · Lead verifies at changeover · HACCP Sec 4.12
01. Open routine — set-up & calibrate
Inspect well oil levels to upper cold scribe mark.
Verify exhaust hoods & makeup-air CFM draw active.
Preheat Wells 1–3 to 350°F; calibrate probe in ice-water (32°F ±1°F).
Set up dredge tubs, ice bath under egg wash (≤ 40°F), sanitized tongs.
Test heat lamp bulbs over fry dump station (≥ 165°F / 74°C).
Stock portion pans, clean landing trays, timer batteries.

02. Mid-shift reset — check, sanitize, stock
Skim crumb surface & floaters with fine mesh skimmer (every 30m in the rush).
Mid-day hot oil filtration on heavy wells (Wells 1 & 2 via pump recirculator).
Top off oil with fresh reserve jug (max 1 qt per well during service).
Wipe crumb trays, splash guards & heat shields (no aerosol sprays near vats).
Restock raw freezer lowboy: chicken, fries, seafood (readout < 0°F; FIFO).
Sanitize seasoning shakers & discard clumped dredge flour (#10 mesh sieve).
RUSH RESET · INIT: [ _____ ] · OIL CHECKED BY EYE [ ]
03. Close breakdown — shutdown & sanitation
Power down burners & pilot valves; cool below 150°F. Do not drain boiling oil.
Run complete filtration through paper & discard cone; replace filter pad envelope.
Scrub vat walls, heating elements & tank sides (high-temp long brass brush).
Empty sediment crumb pans; degrease splash surfaces; air-dry upside down.
Install clean stainless night covers over all 4 wells.
Wipe fry dump station and hang sanitized baskets inverted on wall bracket.

03Clean & hold
Keep it clean
ZERO CROSS-CONTACT Sanitation water: change every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
SEAFOOD Seafood should not be stored above other food.
WARM TABLE 145°F minimum temp. Hold no longer than 4 hours.
04Line discipline
Stop the line
CCP Check internal temp (min 165°F / 74°C). Never pull early. Shake basket to prevent clumping.
Look at the physical basket timer. Never guess.
ALLERGEN Change gloves immediately. Use sanitized RED silicone tongs. Never share fryer oil or staging pan.
BURN HAZ Freeze all line movement. Keep elbows tucked and stay anchored until hot oil transport fully clears corridor.
WASTE Strict dump chute timer audit. Stale fried product must be logged on waste sheet and tossed. Never re-dredge.
04. Oil quality — judge it by eye Card 04 · No meter at this station · Skim frequently
Stage 01Optimal new (day 1–2)
Pale straw
Good for everything · Top off as needed
Light, clean champagne viscosity. Zero off-odors. Minimum crust blistering; optimal for tempura & blanching.
ACTION: Approved for all line items. Top off as needed.
Stage 02Peak quality (day 3–4)
Golden amber
Skim frequently · Skimming buys you days
Perfect heat transfer matrix. Instant seal on proteins. Golden brown crust bloom, crisp cellular bite on dredge.
PRIME WINDOW: Ideal for breaded poultry, hand-cut fries, and seafood.
Stage 03Watch critical (day 5)
Deep hazel / brown
Skim frequently · Filter it today
Persistent surface foam rings. Oil viscosity thickens visibly. Greasy mouthfeel; product begins absorbing up to 18% more oil.
REMEDIATION: Run filter cycle; dump crumb tray; replenish 20% fresh oil.
Stage 04Mandatory discard
Black molasses
Stop — it is done
Lock out the well · Nobody eats this
Acrid white haze. Bitter, scorched volatile hydrocarbon aroma. Darkening core; unapproved for consumption under food code.
MANDATORY: LOCK OUT WELL. Immediate pump-out and caustic boil-out.
Cleaning & reset · every shift
Skim the wells often: skimming frequently makes the oil last longer. Dump the crumb tray when you filter.
Sanitation water: change every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
Cleaning instructions
4-well fryer: 1. Safety. 2. Drain & filter oil. 3. Clean all surfaces with a sanitized towel, green scrubby if needed. Dry towels are for removing solid food particles before sanitizing.
1-well fryer: quick reference steps to be modeled by trainer and verified by chef.
What success looks like
Sanitary, free of hazards, and stocked up for two days. Then a rush is manageable. Success is good communication and not running out of food.
When you're not making food Sanitize, organize, stock up, support, sweep. Nothing else to do? Help somebody with a cleaning project.
