How to print

Chrome or Edge · backgrounds on

Open the page, press Print

Use the Print button on the page, the Print link above, or Ctrl+P. Cards come out Letter landscape; the quick guide comes out Letter portrait. That is set on the page, not by you.

Check two settings

Paper: Letter. "Background graphics": on, so the black banners and hazard stripes print. Leave margins on the default and scale on 100%.

Cut, laminate, punch

For the binder's half-sheet cards, print "2 pages per sheet" and cut the sheet in half, or trim a single landscape print. Laminate in 5 mil matte. Punch the left edge: the card keeps a 1.75 rem strip clear for the holes.

Save as PDF works the same way: choose "Save as PDF" as the printer and hand the file to a print shop. One card, one PDF.

  • 1Hazard stripe, then the ink header bar with the card number, title, revision and paper format.
  • 2The critical control point in critical red, right under the title. Temperatures in °F with °C in brackets.
  • 3The body: two to four columns of panels, each with a station banner, one metric badge and a short checklist. One card, one topic, one side. Never spill to the back.
  • 4Sign-off strip: initials, time or date boxes the lead fills with a grease pencil.
  • 5Footer with the binder spec. The left 1.75 rem stays empty for the three punch holes.
  • 0Corners are square. Shadows are hard offsets or nothing. Borders are 1, 2 or 3 px of ink, amber or red. Nothing is decorative.
