STAGE 01 PALE STRAW / 02 GOLDEN AMBERI doWe doYou do
Point & say01 PALE STRAW, DAY 1-2, good for everything. Light, clean champagne viscosity. Zero off-odors. Approved for all line items. Top off as needed. 02 GOLDEN AMBER, DAY 3-4, SKIM FREQUENTLY. Perfect heat transfer matrix. Ideal for breaded poultry, hand-cut fries, and seafood.

STAGE 03 DEEP HAZEL / BROWN — WATCH (DAY 5)I doWe doYou do
Point & saySKIM FREQUENTLY, FILTER IT TODAY. Persistent surface foam rings. Oil viscosity thickens visibly. REMEDIATION: Run filter cycle; dump crumb tray; replenish 20% fresh oil.
WhyGreasy mouthfeel; product begins absorbing up to 18% more oil.

STAGE 04 BLACK MOLASSES — STOP, IT IS DONEI doWe doYou do
Point & sayLOCK OUT THE WELL. NOBODY EATS THIS. Acrid white haze. Bitter, scorched volatile hydrocarbon aroma. LOCK OUT WELL. Immediate pump-out and caustic boil-out.
WhyDarkening core; unapproved for consumption under food code.

4
FRYER CLEANING & RESET · EVERY SHIFTI doWe doYou do
Point & saySkim the wells often: skimming frequently makes the oil last longer. Dump the crumb tray when you filter. Sanitation water: change every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
What success looks like: sanitary, free of hazards, and stocked up for two days. Success is good communication and not running out of food.

Cleaning instructions (4 well / 1 well)I doWe doYou do
Point & say4 well fryer: 1. Safety. 2. Drain & filter oil. 3. Clean all surfaces with a sanitized towel, green scrubby if needed. Dry towels are for removal of solid food particles prior to sanitization. 1 well fryer: modeled by trainer, verified by chef.
