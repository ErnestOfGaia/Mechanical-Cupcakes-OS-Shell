science
why does polar matter matter? Fresh cooking oil consists almost entirely of non-polar triglycerides (fats). As the oil is exposed to high heat, oxygen, and moisture; they breakdown into polar compounds.  The higher the percentage of polar matter in your fryer, the more degraded and chemically broken down the oil is.
