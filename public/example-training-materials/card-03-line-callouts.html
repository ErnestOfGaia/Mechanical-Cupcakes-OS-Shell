CCP

Check internal temp (min 165°F / 74°C). Never pull early. Shake basket to prevent clumping.

Look at physical basket countdown timer. Never guess or assume remaining seconds.

ALLERGEN

Change gloves immediately. Use sanitized RED silicone tongs. Never share fryer oil or staging pan.

BURN HAZ

Freeze all line movement. Keep elbows tucked and stay anchored until hot oil transport fully clears corridor.

WASTE

Strict dump chute timer audit. Stale fried product must be logged on waste sheet and tossed. Never re-dredge.
