01 STOCK // WHAT FULL LOOKS LIKE

Full Is A Number

SEASON SUMMER / WINTER
check_box Every item has a full level, and it is not the same in summer as in winter.
check_box Simple mnemonics, so nobody has to memorise a number.
check_box The table below is filled in at the station.
help Ask, don't memorise
How long does a pan of this last, and how long does the ingredient itself last?
02 COVER // TWO DAYS

Two Days Of Stock

IF SOMEBODY CALLS IN 2 DAYS
check_box Do I have 2 days of stock if someone doesn't show up tomorrow?
check_box Tell the manager when we're low on an item.
check_box Set the next shift up for success.
help The arithmetic
Change a pan twice a day and want 2 days of stock? That's 4 pans, the line plus the backup.
03 THE WEEK // BUSY OR SLOW

Friday Busy, Tuesday Slow

WHICH WAY FRI / TUE
check_box Going into the weekend you can hardly make too many backups: what isn't used Friday gets used Saturday or Sunday.
check_box From Sunday you're heading toward Tuesday. Monday and Tuesday are slow, so stop stocking up.
check_box Run down what's there. First in, first out.
help Ask yourself
Are we closer to Tuesday or Friday? Delivery days to be confirmed at the station.
04 YOURSELF // SET UP FOR SUCCESS

Breaks And Maintenance

BEFORE YOU STEP AWAY 1 HOUR STOCKED
check_box Breaks are easiest when your position is covered and the line is deepest.
check_box The station should be stocked for the next hour before you go.
check_box Clear anything urgent first, then take the break.
help Before peak
Sanitation water: change every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
