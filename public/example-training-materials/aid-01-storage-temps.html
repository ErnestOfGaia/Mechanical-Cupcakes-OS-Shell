WhyLower basket slowly to prevent splashing. Agitate to avoid clumping. Max 50%: prevent oil thermal shock & soggy crust.

When you're not making food: ask, don't tellEvery answer is on the card
What's the freezer's TARGET TEMP, and what if it's beeping? < 31°F (<1°C). If Freezer is Beeping: temp is low, notify chef, keep closed.
ALLERGEN: what does this card tell you to do? Use gloves and appropriate containers to stay safe.
Dropping a basket: how, and how do you spot an overload? Drop away from torso. Temp drop >25°F = overload.
Three hold limits: the staging pan, the fry holder, the warmer cabinet? 7 MIN MAX HOLD. Fries 20 min. Chicken & tortillas 4 hours.
