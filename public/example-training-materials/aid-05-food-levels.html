WHAT FULL LOOKS LIKEI doWe doYou do
Point & sayEvery item has a full level, and it is not the same in summer as in winter. Simple mnemonics, so nobody has to memorise a number. Ask, don't memorise: how long does a pan of this last, and how long does the ingredient itself last?
CHEF →why it's done this way Q05.3
2
TWO DAYS OF STOCKI doWe doYou do
Point & sayDo I have 2 days of stock if someone doesn't show up tomorrow? Change a pan twice a day and want 2 days? That's 4 pans, the line plus the backup. Tell the manager when we're low. Set the next shift up for success.
CHEF →why it's done this way Q05.4
3
FRIDAY BUSY, TUESDAY SLOWI doWe doYou do
Point & sayGoing into the weekend you can hardly make too many backups: what isn't used Friday gets used Saturday or Sunday. From Sunday you're heading toward Tuesday. Monday and Tuesday are slow, so stop stocking up. Run down what's there, first in first out.
CHEF →why it's done this way Q05.5
4
BREAKS & MAINTENANCEI doWe doYou do
Point & sayBreaks are easiest when your position is covered and the line is deepest. The station should be stocked for the next hour before you go. Clear anything urgent first. Sanitation water: change every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
Offer to cover so they can do station maintenance ahead of peak. Ask what they need before you tell them what to do.

ALSO TODAY · NOT ON THE CARDI doWe doYou do
Point & sayCheck what they think needs stocking, and when the sanitation water was last changed. Offer to cover so they can do station maintenance ahead of peak. Ask what they need before you tell them what to do. Breaks: if somebody leaves at 7 and it's 6, and it's been an hour and a half since your last break, stock your line and ask now, so 10 to 30 minutes can be found. Nobody writes this down and every manager runs it differently, so say it out loud.
When you're not making food: ask, don't tellEvery answer is on the card
Someone calls in sick tomorrow. Are we still stocked up? 2 DAYS OF STOCK. A pan changed twice a day means 4 pans, line plus backup.
Are we closer to Tuesday or Friday, and what changes? FRIDAY BUSY, TUESDAY SLOW. Build backups into the weekend; run them down toward Tuesday, first in first out.
What has to be true before you take a break? Position covered, line stocked for the next hour, urgent things cleared.
How often does the sanitation water change? Every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
