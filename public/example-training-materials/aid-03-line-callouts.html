When you're not making food: ask, don't tellEvery answer is on the card
Sanitize, organize, stock up, support, sweep. Nothing else to do? Help somebody with a cleaning project. First three are for you and your station. Support and sweep are what you do for everybody else.
You hear "I need a chicken strip on the fly please!" What do you call back? "Heard, 6 minutes!"
How often does the sanitation water change, and are dry towels sanitary? Every 2 hours. Dry towels are not sanitary.
What's the minimum internal temp before you pull? CCP: min 165°F / 74°C. Never pull early.
ALLERGEN: what does the card tell you to do? Change gloves immediately. Sanitized RED tongs. Never share fryer oil or staging pan.
You hear "Behind you / Hot oil!" What do you do? Freeze all line movement. Elbows tucked, anchored until hot oil clears corridor.
